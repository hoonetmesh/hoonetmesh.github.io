📡 SIGNAL CONDITIONS

The RF skies over New England were cool and crackly yesterday, with an average RSSI of -113 dBm and an SNR averaging -13.9 dB — not ideal, but certainly workable.

Most packets were classified as very weak (1,396) or weak (1,868), with only 307 strong signals (better than -100 dBm). Just 8 packets earned the coveted good signal label, but those included standouts like:

  • HOONET-TANGO (-27 dBm, SNR +7.8 dB)
  • HOONET-DUNCAN (-28 dBm, SNR +6.2 dB)

These co-located test nodes predictably dominated the strong signal list, but distant nodes like Meshtastic 386c still managed -64 dBm, proving multi-hop resilience.

Propagation showed robust 3-hop (655) and 4-hop (598) performance, with 341 zero-hop signals indicating strong local presence too.
