Signal Conditions – Foggy Skies, Bright Spots

Sunday’s RF landscape was a mixed bag — mostly weak, with a few beacons cutting through the noise.

We recorded 1,337 packets with valid signal data, showing an average RSSI of -113 dBm and SNR of -9.0 dB, both in the fair-to-poor range. A foggy RF environment, if you will.

Only 77 packets registered as strong (≥ -100 dBm), and just 5 hit the Good mark. Most of the traffic — 779 packets — fell in the Weak range, with 276 packets barely audible as Very Weak (RSSI < -120).

But it wasn’t all misty static. Our own HOONET-UNCANOONUC stood out like a mountaintop lighthouse, averaging a toasty -32 dBm with an SNR of 6.7 dB. Other bright spots included MahtRak (-92 dBm) and KC1VOA (-97 dBm).

Multi-hop routing confirmed the mesh is still routing long-distance: 68% of packets traveled 2 hops or more, with 326 packets using exactly 2 hops. The network remains resilient, even when signals are faint.
